Car insurance claims

Extensive exploratory data analysis on car insurance data. Two models - Poisson GLM and XGBoost - are used to predict the number of claims. Models performances are studied and compared against each other.

02

Cost-per-click surge attribution

Adapted from a real business scenario. A +18% cost-per-click surge is observed in aggregated data for 1,900+ digital advertising campaigns. Rate-mix decomposition is used to properly attribute the contribution of each individual campaign to the global figure.

Data shape under Neural Networks

Demonstration of how the shape of a data cloud simplifies as it progresses through the layers of a neural network. A small section on persistent homology is included. Inspired by the paper "Topology of deep neural networks".
